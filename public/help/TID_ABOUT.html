Bug reporting.

Software is not supposed to have bugs in it, so if a program crashes or has functional errors it means the Engineer made a mistake. Its not "just one of those things" - unlike most (all?) other technical disciplines, Software Engineering is completely deterministic and predictable.

So. I'm not saying this software is perfect - I may well have made some mistakes. If so, I want you to report them to me so I can correct them. If I don't know about 'em, I can't fix 'em. (I'd also like to hear any ideas you may have for making Traxx even better...)

Please try your best to remember what you were doing when the problem occured - maybe even send me the file, if applicable - when reporting bugs.

Send bug reports to the PO Box address above or (preferably) e-mail to SoftParts1@aol.com.
